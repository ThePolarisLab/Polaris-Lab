# PGE-009.8 — Executive Projection Engine

## Purpose

The Executive Projection Engine converts provider evidence into stable, provider-neutral executive entities. Connectors remain responsible for collection; projections are responsible for interpretation; the Executive Read Model remains responsible for business-facing state.

## Architecture

```text
Hermes EvidenceEnvelope
        |
        v
ExecutiveProjectionEngine
        |
        +-- QuickBooksFinancialProjection
        +-- MotiveVehicleProjection
        +-- OutlookTaskProjection
        |
        v
ExecutiveRepository
        |
        v
Executive Query and Briefing Services
```

## Guarantees

- deterministic projection ordering by priority and projection id
- projection registration uniqueness
- evidence idempotency within an engine lifecycle
- organization isolation for entities and events
- optimistic entity version increments
- evidence and external-reference preservation
- provider-specific payloads do not enter the Executive Read Model contracts

## Initial projections

### QuickBooks

Profit and loss, cash flow, AR aging, and AP aging evidence incrementally update one organization financial snapshot.

### Motive

Vehicle and vehicle-utilization evidence update provider-neutral executive vehicle entities.

### Outlook

Messages explicitly marked as requiring follow-up create executive tasks while retaining message evidence.

## Deferred

- durable idempotency ledger
- projection checkpoints and replay orchestration
- customer and driver entity resolution
- conflict policies across multiple sources
- dead-letter handling
- distributed projection workers
- knowledge graph edges
- Athena reasoning and autonomous actions

## Review gate

CI must pass before merge. Review must confirm that organization isolation, evidence provenance, idempotency, and the connector/projection/read-model boundary remain intact.
